A letter from the founder

Dear customer

thank you for taking a moment to read a few words about us. We started twelve years ago in Poprad with one van and one rule: every job is handed over in person.

Today we are a team of six and that rule still holds. We do not work with anonymous clients and we do not chase the lowest price. We aim for work we can stand behind even five years from now.
